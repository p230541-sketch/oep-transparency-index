Before you pay a recruitment agent —

Check the agency’s official record.

Pakistan’s Bureau of Emigration publishes every complaint, warning, suspension and blacklisting against licensed overseas employment promoters — but only as a news feed nobody can search. This register re-indexes those official notices, agency by agency.
