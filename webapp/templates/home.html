Check a recruitment agency's official record before you pay them.

Pakistan's Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment publishes every complaint, warning, and blacklisting against licensed overseas employment promoters (OEPs) — but only as a news feed nobody can search. This site re-indexes those official notices so you can look up one agency and see its complete track record.

How it works

  1. Type the agency's name or its OEPL licence number.
  2. Open the agency's profile to see every official action, newest first.
  3. Every entry links to the original notice on beoe.gov.pk — verify everything yourself.
